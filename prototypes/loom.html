Turquoise News

News from and about the Turkic world, in its own languages, from people who live there. In weavers' lore the bird brings news from far away. Turquoise is the Turkish stone: Europe named it for the lands it came through.

Yıldız, the star

TurkicBlue Research Network

Linguists, historians, archaeologists and social scientists working on the Turkic world: shared sources, open datasets, peer review, and a home for work that falls between national academies. The star is good fortune, and a light to steer by.

How it will work

Weave your own motif

Kilim motifs are mirrored four ways. Draw one quarter on the grid, and the loom repeats it into a rug.

When the DAO opens, each member will add a motif to one shared kilim. [Coming with the DAO]

What the threads hold together

  • PreserveRecord history, language and tradition before they are lost: epics, songs, recipes, inscriptions and the people who remember them.
  • RepresentShow the Turkic world as its own people know it, in their own languages and scripts.
  • SpreadMake that knowledge free to read, cite and share, under an open licence.
  • CelebrateGive music, craft, food, festivals and new work the attention they deserve.
  • ProtectAnswer misinformation with sources, and defend the reputation and rights of Turkic peoples.

Decided hand in hand

For centuries Turkic peoples settled the big questions in the kurultay, an assembly of everyone with a stake. The TurkicBlue DAO brings that to the foundation, on Ethereum, where every decision stays on the record.

Membership: [token or non-transferable badge, to be decided]. Opens: [date to be announced].

  1. ProposeAny member can put forward a project, a grant or a change to the rules.
  2. DebateThe proposal is discussed in the open, in any of our languages.
  3. VoteMembers vote on chain. The result is public and final.
  4. Carry outThe foundation does the work and reports back on what the money did.
