In the 730s the Kök Türks carved their history into standing stones at Orkhon and called them bengü taş, the eternal stones. TurkicBlue is an international community doing the same for today: preserving, representing and celebrating Turkic culture, history and knowledge, and standing up for the reputation and rights of Turkic peoples.

The kurultay

One stone becomes many

The TurkicBlue DAO is a kurultay on Ethereum, the old Turkic assembly rebuilt for the internet: every member a stone in the circle, every decision voted on in the open.

Kök means blue, and it means sky.

In Old Turkic one word names both, and Tengri, the sky, was the highest god. The Kök Türks took the word as their name. Ours comes from theirs.

The colour has stayed. Look at the flags Turkic peoples fly today, and the sky is still there.

  • Kazakhstansky blue, with a golden sun and steppe eagle
  • Azerbaijanblue for Turkic heritage, the top of three stripes
  • Uzbekistana blue band with crescent and twelve stars
  • Crimean Tatarslight blue with a golden tamga
  • Uyghursthe Kökbayraq, literally the blue flag
  • Gagauzblue, white and red, with three stars

Five words we carve into everything we do

  • PreserveRecord history, language and tradition before they are lost.
  • RepresentShow the Turkic world as its own people know it, in their own languages.
  • SpreadMake that knowledge free to read, cite and share.
  • CelebrateGive music, craft, food and festivals the attention they deserve.
  • ProtectAnswer misinformation with sources, and defend the rights of Turkic peoples.

How the kurultay will decide

For centuries Turkic peoples settled the big questions in the kurultay, an assembly of everyone with a stake. The TurkicBlue DAO brings that to the foundation, on Ethereum, where every vote stays on the record.
