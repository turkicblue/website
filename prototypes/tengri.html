About the foundation

Turkic Blue Foundation is a non-profit organisation run by an international community of volunteers, researchers and supporters.

We document and share the culture, history and languages of the Turkic peoples, from the Balkans to Siberia, and we work to make this knowledge free and accurate. We also respond to misinformation and stand up for the reputation and rights of Turkic communities.

Our work is open: content is published under free licences, and decisions about funding will be made publicly by the foundation's members.

How governance will work

Why "Blue"

The Old Turkic word kök means both blue and the sky. The Göktürks, whose khaganate stretched across Central Asia in the 6th to 8th centuries, called themselves Kök Türük.

The same word is used across the Turkic languages today, in Latin, Cyrillic and Arabic scripts. Our name follows it.

Governance

The foundation will be governed by its members through a DAO, a decentralised autonomous organisation, on the Ethereum blockchain.

Members will decide how funds are spent and which projects the foundation supports. Every proposal, vote and payment will be public. The model follows the kurultay, the assembly in which Turkic peoples historically made major decisions together.

  1. ProposalA member submits a proposal: a project, a grant or a rule change.
  2. DiscussionMembers discuss it openly, in any of the working languages.
  3. VoteMembers vote on chain. The result is binding and public.
  4. ImplementationThe foundation carries it out and reports on the outcome.
